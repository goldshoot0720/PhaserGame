# Style guide

- Wide 2400-px side-view field; sky gradient, parallax clouds and hills, layered grass/dirt terrain drawn as filled polygons; blue water at the bottom.
- Three map palettes: grassland, snowfield, desert.
- The eight 萌友 chibis ride tinted copies of one generated toy-tank hull; heart supply barrels drop from the sky.
- Traditional Chinese HUD: tank cards, wind gauge, turn clock, minimap, weapon slots, angle and power bar.
