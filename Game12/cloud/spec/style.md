# Style guide

- 15×13 grass-checker board with a gold rim; pastel cottage pillars and ribbon crates drawn row-sorted with players.
- Blue water streams with white highlights; trapped players float in a shiny bubble with a countdown.
- Generated items: balloon, potion (range), roller skate (speed), needle (rescue), red potion (max range).
- Side cards for all four players (stats, wins, status). Traditional Chinese UI.
