First release of 萌友大亂鬥: an 8-player top-down free-for-all arena shooter — pick a chibi fighter with a signature weapon (bubble cannon, SMG, sniper, shotgun, boomerang, spell pages, triple fire, splash grenade), mouse-aim and dash, grab heart and power pickups, bots that hunt, strafe and lead shots, kill feed with portraits, minimap, Tab scoreboard; first to 10 kills or most kills in 3 minutes.
